~/about

I'm Aakash — a final-year BCA student from Ranchi, and an Arch Linux devotee. In 2023, I bought my first laptop. Six months later, Windows had quietly eaten most of my system's resources. I installed Arch Linux. I never looked back.

Over the next year, I tested 14 distros — Arch, NixOS, Gentoo, Kali, BlackArch, Fedora, Debian, and more. Each one taught me something different about control, transparency, and how a system should be built.

Now I'm building Umbrella-Corporation_OS (a custom Arch ISO with Red Queen aesthetics), running a self-hosted homelab that replaced all my subscriptions, and working toward my first Linux Engineer role.

Discipline: 2-hour deep work cap · Daily 5km run · Learning Russian · Progress is a system, not a burst.
